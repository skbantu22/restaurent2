Welcome to the Gate

From Dhaka to Forest Gate

At Shawon Food Gate you'll find Bangladeshi home cooking, Lahori curries, Turkish döner and a proper British breakfast together under one roof on Forest Lane.

Start the day with a Sylheti Breakfast Thali and Deshi chai. For lunch there's a Peri Rice Chicken Bowl, and in the evening you can share a Grill Platter with the family. On Fridays and Saturdays, don't miss our slow-cooked Sultan's Kacchi Biryani.
